    📈Reading the market's mood

    Markets move on words.
    We read them for you.

    A language model reads the financial news, labels every headline positive, neutral or negative, and turns thousands of them into one signal per stock. This prototype tracks seven tech stocks to keep things simple.

    Powered by FinBERT ↗
    Why we built this

    Whoever reads the news first gets the edge.

    This project started with a New York Times story about access to Truth Social's API. Early access to market-moving news lets a few players place winning bets before everyone else has even seen the headline.

    We're students, and we don't have $100K lying around for that kind of access. So we built what we could: a pipeline that collects free market news, a model that reads every headline, and a dashboard that turns them into a clear signal for each stock.

    A proof of concept, not a trading tool. We use free news APIs, so headlines arrive hours late rather than in real time. With a paid, real-time feed, the same pipeline could score news the moment it breaks. FinBERT is just our starting model; stronger models can be dropped in through the same promotion rule.
    The story that started it The New York Times · July 16, 2026 ↗
    How it works
    1. Collect. Airflow pulls news from the free Finnhub and Marketaux APIs, checks every batch against a schema, and versions each snapshot with DVC.
    2. Read. A language model, FinBERT for now, labels each headline for its company, with a confidence score and the words that drove it.
    3. Prove. A new model replaces the champion only if it's significantly better on held-out news and no stock gets worse.
    4. Show. This dashboard: daily sentiment against the price, a two-week market pulse, and the reasoning behind every label.

    Promotion rule
    • Validate. Macro F1 of at least 0.60 on the gold set, or the run fails.
    • A real gain. The macro F1 gain stays above zero across a 95% bootstrap interval, so luck can't promote a model.
    • No ticker worse off. No ticker more than 0.02 less accurate than under the champion.
    • Ship or roll back. A winner is copied to cloud storage, then becomes the champion; otherwise the champion stays live.
